Sweet Dreams is an upcoming 3D horror game by Dark Echo Studios .

Players take the role of a young man investigating the abandoned Sweet Dreams Orphanage after receiving a cryptic message. Explore the facility, solve environmental puzzles, and collect forgotten recordings to uncover the building’s dark history.

The game features a distorting reality haunted by a mysterious presence that hinders escape. Developed in the Godot engine, currently in early stages, planned for a future Windows release.
