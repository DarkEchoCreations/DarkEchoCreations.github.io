🎮 About

Sweet Dreams is a story-driven 3D horror game currently in development.

You take on the role of a young man whose ordinary life takes an unexpected turn after discovering the mysterious Sweet Dreams Orphanage.

What begins as a simple investigation slowly turns into something much darker.

«The deeper you go, the harder it becomes to tell what is real.»

---

📖 Story

After receiving a mysterious message connected to the abandoned Sweet Dreams Orphanage, you decide to investigate.

The building has been abandoned for years.

Yet something inside is still waiting.

Strange recordings, forgotten documents, unexplained phenomena and a mysterious presence begin revealing what happened to the people who once lived there.

And eventually, you discover that the orphanage may not be as abandoned as everyone thought.

---

👤 Main Character

The Protagonist

You play as a young man who becomes involved in the mystery surrounding Sweet Dreams.

Your investigation will take you through abandoned rooms, hidden areas and places that were never meant to be discovered.

---

🏚️ Sweet Dreams Orphanage

Once a place meant to give children a home.

👁️ The Unknown

Something is wrong with Sweet Dreams.

🛠️ Development

Sweet Dreams is an independent game currently being developed using:

- 🎮 Godot
- 🧊 Blender
- 🎨 Custom 3D assets
- 🔊 Custom audio & atmosphere
- 💻 GDScript

The game is still in development, so features, story elements and gameplay mechanics may change.
